# Wiki

Use this folder as the durable project wiki. It should contain confirmed project facts distilled from source input, stakeholder confirmation, and generated delivery outputs.

Organize wiki pages into knowledge-area folders. The starter folders are placeholders; rename them to project-specific areas when the project shape is known.

## Knowledge Areas

* [Knowledge Area 1](knowledge-area-1/) - Placeholder for one durable wiki area such as business rules, workflows, system behavior, or operational notes.
* [Knowledge Area 2](knowledge-area-2/) - Placeholder for another durable wiki area such as known issues, limitations, decisions, or support notes.
* [Shared Diagrams](diagrams/) - Copied diagrams that support more than one wiki knowledge area.

Store these topics here instead of keeping separate top-level folders:

* Business rules, validations, permissions, and policy decisions.
* System behavior, workflow behavior, state transitions, exceptions, and integration behavior.
* Known issues, current limitations, unsupported scenarios, and accepted workarounds.
* Important notes, assumptions, dependencies, decisions, risks, and stakeholder caveats.

Do not store generated BA deliverables here. Those belong in `../../requirements/output/`.

For requirement analysis, open this folder only when the deliverable needs durable business rules, behavior, known issues, limitations, notes, scope boundaries, decisions, risks, or dependencies.
